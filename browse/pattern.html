  • 10-foot UIalso ten-foot experience, TV UI, leanback UI, living-room UI

    The register for screens read from a couch: focus-driven navigation with a remote, oversized type and cards, horizontal rails, and safe margins for TV overscan.

  • Achievement badgealso collectible achievements, trophy, achievement, unlock

    A collectible marker awarded for a milestone and displayed on a profile, often shown greyed out before it is earned so the set is visible.

  • Activity feedalso activity stream, news feed, audit log, what's happening

    A reverse chronological list of things that happened, each entry naming an actor, a verb, and an object, grouped when many are alike.

  • Address autocompletealso address lookup, postcode lookup, address finder, autofill address

    Replacing a set of address fields with one lookup that suggests full addresses after a few characters and fills the rest on selection.

  • Anti-patternalso antipattern, design anti-pattern

    A solution reached for again and again for a familiar problem, which fails at the thing it was reached for and often leaves the situation worse than doing nothing.

  • Applied filtersalso filter chips, filter pills, active filters, filter tokens, selected filters

    A row of removable tokens above the results showing every filter currently in force, with a control to clear them all at once.

  • Autofillalso browser autofill, form autofill, autofill suggestions

    The browser filling a form from values it has already saved, on its own or from a dropdown of the reader's own profile, so a field arrives answered.

  • Autoplayalso muted autoplay, automatic playback, autoplay attribute, autoplay policy

    Playback that starts with no gesture from the reader, which browsers now allow only while the sound is off, so autoplay in practice means a silent moving picture.

  • Autosavealso auto-save, saving indicator, all changes saved, draft saving

    Persisting the reader's work continuously without a save action, usually with a quiet status line reporting when the last save happened.

  • Back to topalso scroll to top, return to top, back to top link, float back to top, back to top button, backtop, jump to top

    A control that appears after the reader has scrolled some distance and returns them to the top of the page in one action.

  • Bulk actionsalso batch actions, bulk edit, multi-select actions, contextual action bar, selection toolbar, bulk action bar

    Selecting several rows and applying one command to all of them, usually through a bar that appears once a selection exists and reports its count.

  • Bulletproof buttonalso bulletproof email button, VML button, email button

    A mail button built to survive a renderer that drops CSS: the fill carried by a cell or box behind the link rather than by a background the client can refuse.

  • Chartjunkalso chart junk, non-data ink

    Tufte's word for ink that decorates a chart without carrying data: 3D bevels, gradient fills, mascots, gridline thickets.

  • Check answersalso review page, summary page, check your answers, order review, confirm and submit

    A review screen listing everything the reader entered, with a change link beside each answer, shown before the submission is final.

  • Comparison preventionalso price comparison prevention, obfuscated pricing, incomparable tiers

    Structuring plans or products so their features and prices cannot be lined up, forcing a decision that no side-by-side reading would support.

  • Confirmation pagealso success page, thank you page, receipt page, order confirmation

    The screen that closes a transaction, stating plainly that it succeeded, giving a reference the reader can quote, and saying what happens next.

  • Confirmshamingalso shame decline, guilt opt-out, no thanks, I hate saving money

    Wording the decline option so that choosing it is an admission of something embarrassing, making refusal feel like a confession rather than a choice.

  • Conversational interfacealso chat interface, conversational UI, chatbot UI

    An interface operated in turns of language rather than by controls, where the transcript is the state and the only visible affordance is a box to type in.

  • Cookie consent banneralso cookie banner, consent notice, CMP, consent management platform, GDPR banner

    A notice asking permission to store non-essential cookies before the reader has done anything, offering accept, reject, and a route to per-purpose choices.

  • Countdown timeralso urgency timer, offer expires in, session timer, seat hold timer, limited duration

    A running clock attached to an offer or a held resource, showing how long remains and what will happen when it reaches zero.

  • Cursor paginationalso keyset pagination, cursor-based pagination, seek pagination, next and previous only

    Paging that moves by a pointer into the result set rather than a page number, so newly inserted rows never shift what the next page contains.

  • Dark mode emailalso email dark mode, dark mode for email

    Designing a mail for clients that will invert its palette without asking, where a near-black behaves better than black and a logo needs a ground it survives on.

  • Dark patternalso deceptive design, deceptive pattern, manipulative design

    An interface arranged to make the reader do something they did not intend and would not have chosen, by exploiting how attention and defaults work.

  • Data-ink ratioalso data-ink, data to ink ratio

    Tufte's measure of chart economy: the share of ink that would be missed if erased, with everything else a candidate for deletion.

  • Did you meanalso spelling suggestion, query correction, showing results for, search instead for

    Offering a corrected spelling for a query that returned little or nothing, either as a suggestion to accept or as a substitution the reader can undo.

  • Diegetic interfacealso diegetic UI, in-world UI, non-diegetic UI, meta UI, spatial game UI

    An interface that exists inside the fiction it serves, so the character can see and use it too, rather than being drawn on top of the world for the player alone.

  • Direct labelingalso direct labels, data labels, value labels

    Writing series names next to the data instead of in a legend, trading a lookup for a glance.

  • Direct manipulationalso in-place manipulation, WYSIWYG, handles and grips, on-canvas editing

    Acting on an object by handling the object itself, dragging, resizing, or typing into it, rather than describing the change in a separate control.

  • Disguised adalso native ad, sponsored content, fake download button, ad camouflage

    An advertisement styled to look like a control or an item of content, so the click it collects was aimed at something else entirely.

  • Double opt-inalso confirmed opt-in, subscription confirmation

    Asking a new subscriber to confirm from the address they typed, so a list holds only addresses whose owner agreed and a typo never becomes a stranger's mail.

  • Drag previewalso drag overlay, drag image, ghost image, drag ghost

    The semi-transparent copy of the dragged item that follows the pointer, sometimes collapsed into a stack when several items move at once.

  • Drag to reorderalso sortable list, reorderable list, drag and drop reordering, rearrange

    Changing the order of a list by dragging an item to a new position, with the remaining items shifting to show where it will land.

  • Drill-down navigationalso panel stack, nested panel navigation, push navigation, drilldown

    Going deeper into a hierarchy one level at a time inside the same panel, each level replacing the last and offering a way back up.

  • Drip pricingalso hidden costs, partitioned pricing, junk fees, resort fee

    Advertising a low headline price and adding mandatory fees one screen at a time, so the true total only appears once the buyer is committed.

  • Drop indicatoralso insertion indicator, drop line, drop target highlight, placeholder gap

    The line or highlighted gap drawn during a drag to show exactly where the item will land if it is released now.

  • Dual axisalso secondary axis, dual y-axis, two y-axes, combo chart

    Two value axes sharing one plot so two series can be compared, and a free knob for manufacturing whatever correlation you like.

  • Empty statealso non-ideal state, blankslate, blank slate, blank state

    The screen a container shows when it holds nothing, naming the absence and offering the action that ends it.

  • Endowed progressalso endowed progress effect, artificial advancement, head start progress, upfront progress

    Starting a progress meter part filled, so the reader feels they are continuing something already begun rather than starting from nothing.

  • Error pagealso 404 page, page not found, not found page, error screen, 500 page, server error page

    The whole screen a site serves instead of the page that was asked for: what happened, in the reader's words, and the shortest way back to something that works.

  • Error summaryalso error list, validation summary, there is a problem, form error summary

    A block at the top of a failed form listing every problem as links that move focus to the field that caused it.

  • Express checkoutalso wallet buttons, one-click checkout, accelerated checkout, buy now button, payment request

    A row of one-tap payment buttons placed above the normal form, which fetch address and payment details from a wallet the buyer already has.

  • Faceted searchalso faceted navigation, guided navigation, filter sidebar, refinements, faceted filters

    Narrowing results by ticking values from several independent attribute groups, with counts showing how many results each value would leave.

  • Fake dooralso fake door test, fake door testing, painted door, painted door test

    A control that looks shipped and leads nowhere, put in the interface on purpose to count how many people want the feature behind it.

  • Fake scarcityalso false scarcity, invented stock count, high demand badge

    Claiming limited supply that is not real, with stock counts or demand messages generated to pressure a decision rather than report a fact.

  • Fake social proofalso fake reviews, activity notification spam, fabricated testimonials

    Manufacturing evidence that other people approve, through invented reviews, testimonials, or live activity messages nobody actually generated.

  • Fake urgencyalso false urgency, resetting countdown, perpetual sale

    Presenting a deadline that does not exist, usually a timer that resets on reload, so the reader hurries a decision they had time to make.

  • False bottomalso false floor, illusion of completeness, premature end, suggesting continuity

    A page that looks finished at a point where content actually continues, so the reader stops scrolling and never sees the rest.

  • Feature gatealso upgrade prompt, freemium wall, locked feature, teaser lock, pro badge

    Showing a locked feature in place, with its real position in the interface, so the reader sees what upgrading would unlock rather than a hidden absence.

  • Feature spotlightalso what's new, release announcement, new feature callout, announcing new features, changelog popover

    A one-time announcement pointing at something newly added, shown in place next to the feature and dismissed for good once acknowledged.

  • First run experiencealso FRE, first-time user experience, FTUE, welcome flow, sample data, onboarding flow

    Everything shown only on the very first open, from a welcome sequence to seeded sample content, and never shown to that person again.

  • Forced continuityalso hidden subscription, auto-renewal trap, negative option billing, free trial trap

    Turning a free trial into a paid subscription automatically, with the card taken up front and no reminder before the first real charge.

  • Forgiving formatalso forgiving input, lenient parsing, flexible input, input normalisation

    Accepting whatever shape the reader types and normalising it afterwards, instead of rejecting input that differs only in spacing or punctuation.

  • Friend spamalso contact list harvesting, invite spam, invite friends, address book import

    Asking for access to a contact list under one pretext and using it to send invitations that appear to come from the reader.

  • Generative UIalso GenUI, AI-native UI, AI-generated interface, agent-native UI, adaptive interface

    An interface whose parts are chosen or composed by a model at runtime, so a request is answered with a built form, chart, or panel instead of a paragraph of text.

  • Goal gradientalso goal-gradient effect, almost there, two stamps to go

    Effort rising as a visible goal gets closer, which is why interfaces show how little is left rather than how much has been done.

  • Gradual engagementalso gradual sign up, progressive engagement, value first onboarding

    Replacing a sign-up form at the front door with a first useful action, so the reader experiences value before being asked for anything.

  • Guest checkoutalso checkout as guest, no account checkout, anonymous checkout

    Letting a buyer complete a purchase without creating an account, with the offer to save details moved to after the order is placed.

  • Help draweralso help panel, contextual help, info panel, side sheet help

    Documentation opened beside the task rather than over it, keeping the form usable while the explanation is read and staying open across steps.

  • Honeypot fieldalso honeypot, bot trap field, spam trap input

    A form field hidden from people but visible to automated form fillers, used to reject submissions that fill it in without challenging anyone.

  • Hub and spokealso hub-and-spoke navigation, springboard, launchpad navigation, home link

    A navigation shape where every task is reached from one home screen and returns to it, with no way to move sideways between tasks.

  • Hydrationalso rehydration, client-side hydration

    The pass where script attaches behaviour to server-rendered HTML, turning a page that is already painted and readable into one that answers input.

  • Infinite scrollalso endless scroll, continuous scrolling, infinite scrolling, auto-load, infinite list

    A list that appends the next page of results automatically as the reader approaches the bottom, so the content never ends and no page controls appear.

  • Inline autocompletealso autocomplete inline, inline completion, ghost text, type-ahead completion, URL bar autofill

    Completing the reader's text inside the field itself, with the guessed remainder selected so the next keystroke replaces it.

  • Inline validationalso live validation, real-time validation, as-you-type validation, input feedback

    Checking a field as the reader fills or leaves it and showing the verdict beside that field, rather than saving every complaint for the submit button.

  • Interest invokeralso interestfor, interesttarget, interest target, hover card trigger

    A link or button that shows more when a reader shows interest in it, by hovering, focusing or long pressing, with no click and no script deciding what interest means.

  • Interface metaphoralso desktop metaphor, UI metaphor, conceptual metaphor

    Explaining a system by dressing it as something the user already knows: the desktop, the trash can, the folder, the shopping cart.

  • Interstitialalso interruption page, interstitial ad, splash interstitial, in-between screen

    A full screen placed between two steps of a flow, carrying a message, an advert, or a warning that the reader must pass through to continue.

  • Invoker commandalso commandfor, command attribute, invokers

    A button that names the element it acts on and what to do to it, in markup, so opening a dialog or toggling a popover needs no script at all.

  • Labor illusionalso fake loading bar, faux progress bar, artificial delay, labour illusion

    Showing the work a system is doing, or stretching it, so the answer feels earned: the search that lists the sites it is checking instead of answering at once.

  • Lazy loadingalso deferred loading, import on visibility, below the fold loading, loading=lazy

    Deferring the fetch of an image, list section, or module until it is about to be needed, usually when it approaches the viewport.

  • Lazy registrationalso deferred registration, anonymous account, try before you sign up, guest mode

    Letting someone use a product and accumulate state anonymously, and asking for an account only when that state needs to survive the session.

  • Leaderboardalso ranking table, high score table, podium, top contributors

    A ranked list of participants by some score, with the reader's own position pinned into view even when it falls outside the visible range.

  • List virtualizationalso windowing, virtual scrolling, virtualized list, virtual list, row virtualization

    Rendering only the rows currently in view and a small buffer around them, while spacers preserve the scrollbar of the full list.

  • Load morealso show more button, load more button, manual pagination

    A button at the end of a list that fetches and appends the next batch only when pressed, keeping the reader in control of how much arrives.

  • Magic linkalso email link sign-in, passwordless link, login link, check your email screen

    Signing in by clicking a single-use link emailed on request, with no password, and a screen that waits and offers to resend while the mail arrives.

  • Mention autocompletealso at-mention, @mention, user tagging, trigger character menu

    Typing a trigger character inside a text field to open a filtered list of people or objects, inserting the chosen one as a linked token.

  • Metered paywallalso soft paywall, freemium meter, article counter, leaky paywall

    A paywall that grants a fixed number of free items per period and counts down publicly, so the barrier arrives only after the reader is invested.

  • Microcopyalso UX writing, UX copy, UI copy, interface copy, product copy

    An interface's smallest strings: the verb on the button, the line under the field, the sentence when it fails. Short enough to be read every time.

  • Microsurveyalso in-app survey, one-question survey, NPS prompt, feedback mechanism, was this helpful

    A one or two question survey placed inside the product at the moment being asked about, answered in place rather than on a separate page.

  • Mini cartalso cart drawer, slide-out cart, cart preview, basket flyout

    A compact view of the basket opened from the cart icon, confirming what was just added and offering checkout without leaving the current page.

  • Misdirectionalso visual interference, false hierarchy, attention misdirection, greyed-out decline

    Using contrast, size, and position to pull attention to the option the business prefers while the other option is present but visually demoted.

  • Mockupalso mock-up, mock UI, high-fidelity mockup, hi-fi mockup, visual comp

    A screen shown as it will look rather than as it will work: colour, type and real copy all settled, and nothing in it clickable.

  • Morphing controlalso state-dependent button, multifunctional control

    One control that changes what it says and does according to state, showing play when paused and pause when playing rather than offering both.

  • Multimodal interfacealso multimodal UX, voice-first, voice user interface, VUI, multimodal UI

    An interface that accepts and answers in more than one channel at once, letting a person speak, type, touch, or point and pick whichever suits the moment.

  • Naggingalso persistent prompt, prompt fatigue, repeat ask, maybe later loop

    Interrupting a task again and again with the same request, so that agreeing becomes the cheapest way to be left alone.

  • Natural language interfacealso NLI, natural language UI, NLUI, language user interface

    An interface whose input is ordinary language, typed or spoken: the user states an intent in their own words and the system parses it, instead of picking from controls.

  • No results statealso zero results, no matches, empty search results, null state

    What a search or filtered list shows when nothing matched, naming the query, explaining why, and offering a way to loosen the constraints.

  • Notification digestalso notification grouping, notification bundling, batched notifications, summary notification, notification summary

    Collecting many similar alerts into one summary rather than delivering each, so the count rises but the number of interruptions does not.

  • Obstructionalso friction by design, intentional friction, manage preferences maze

    Putting deliberate hurdles in front of an action the business does not want taken, so the effort itself does the discouraging.

  • Offline indicatoralso connection status bar, you are offline, sync status, stale data indicator, reconnecting banner

    A persistent notice that the product has lost its connection, saying what still works, what is queued, and when the last sync happened.

  • Onboarding checklistalso getting started checklist, setup checklist, task list, complete multiple tasks, quick start guide

    A persistent list of setup tasks with completion state and a progress meter, kept available so the reader can return to unfinished steps.

  • Onboarding touralso product tour, guided tour, walkthrough, playthrough, feature tour, in-app tour

    A short sequence of pointers layered over a live interface, each naming one control, advanced by the reader and skippable at any point.

  • One thing per pagealso question pages, one question per page, single question form

    Asking a single question on each screen of a form so the reader never chooses which field to answer first and errors point somewhere unambiguous.

  • One-time code loginalso OTP, email OTP, verification code, six digit code, two-factor code

    Signing in by typing a short code sent to a second channel, keeping the reader inside the app rather than sending them out to a mail client.

  • Operational transparencyalso visible work, showing the work

    Showing the work as it happens, the kitchen made visible, so a wait is understood rather than merely endured and the result is trusted more for having been watched.

  • Optimistic UIalso optimistic update

    Showing the result of an action immediately and reconciling with the server afterward, instead of making the user wait for confirmation.

  • Order summaryalso order recap, cart summary, basket summary, cost breakdown

    The persistent panel beside a checkout listing items, quantities, and every cost line, kept visible so nothing about the total arrives as a surprise.

  • Overloaded commandalso overloaded control, overloaded icon, overloaded button

    One command word or icon wired to different outcomes depending on where it sits, so having used it once teaches the reader nothing about the next one.

  • Passkeyalso WebAuthn, FIDO2, biometric login, device credential

    Signing in with a device-held credential unlocked by the reader's own biometric or screen lock, so the interface asks for a face or fingerprint, not a secret.

  • Password strength meteralso strength indicator, password meter, password quality bar

    A gauge that scores a password while it is typed and names what would improve it, replacing a list of rules with continuous feedback.

  • Paywallalso hard paywall, content gate, subscription wall, premium wall

    A barrier that stops access to content or a feature until the reader pays, placed either before anything is shown or partway into the content.

  • Permission primingalso pre-permission prompt, soft ask, double permission pattern, primer dialog, permission pre-prompt

    Explaining why access is needed in the product's own dialog first, and only firing the operating system prompt once the reader has said yes to that.

  • Preselected opt-inalso preselection, prechecked box, opt-out by default, default consent

    Arriving at a choice with the answer that benefits the business already ticked, so consent is collected from everyone who does not notice.

  • Preview textalso preheader, preheader text, snippet text

    The line an inbox prints under a subject, written as hidden text at the top of the mail so the client shows those words instead of scraping whatever body copy comes first.

  • Print stylesheetalso print styles, @media print, print view

    The rules that decide what a page becomes on paper: navigation dropped, link targets spelled out, backgrounds abandoned, and the content given page margins.

  • Priority plus navigationalso priority+ navigation, priority plus pattern, more menu overflow nav, adaptive navigation, overflow list

    A horizontal menu that keeps as many items visible as fit and moves the rest into a More control, recalculating whenever the available width changes.

  • Privacy zuckeringalso privacy dark pattern, oversharing by default, consent maze

    Steering people into sharing more about themselves than they meant to, by making the permissive choice the easy one and the private choice several clicks deep.

  • Progressive disclosure

    Showing the few options most people need and keeping the rest one deliberate step away, so complexity arrives only when it is asked for.

  • Progressive image loadingalso blur-up, LQIP, low quality image placeholder, blurhash, dominant colour placeholder

    Showing a cheap stand-in for an image, a blur, a dominant colour, or a low resolution copy, and swapping in the full file once it arrives.

  • Progressive profilingalso incremental profiling, drip profiling, profile completeness

    Collecting profile details a few at a time across separate visits, instead of demanding the whole record in one long form at registration.

  • Progressive reductionalso adaptive simplification, experience-based UI, proficiency-adaptive interface

    Simplifying a control as the same person uses it more, dropping the label once the icon has been learned and restoring it after a long absence.

  • Prototypealso clickable prototype, interactive prototype, high-fidelity prototype, hi-fi prototype, paper prototype, prototyping

    A design wired up just enough to be tried: screens linked so that a tap goes somewhere, with nothing real behind the links.

  • Pull to refreshalso swipe to refresh, drag to refresh, pull down to refresh

    Dragging a list downward past its top edge to trigger a reload, with an indicator that appears in the space the drag opens up.

  • Quick return headeralso hidey bar, headroom, hide on scroll header, auto-hiding header, quick return pattern

    A header that slides out of the way while the reader scrolls down and returns immediately on the first upward scroll, without waiting for the top of the page.

  • Quick viewalso quick look, peek, preview modal, item preview, hover preview

    Opening a summary of an item over the list it came from, so the reader can inspect it and return without losing scroll position or filters.

  • Rage clickalso rage clicking, rage tap, frustration click

    A burst of repeated clicks on one spot, the shape frustration takes when a control answers nothing, and the analytics signal that goes looking for it.

  • Rating promptalso review request, app rating prompt, review gating, rate this app, how are we doing

    An in-product request to rate or review, usually timed to a moment of success and often filtered so only positive responses reach the public store.

  • Read receiptalso delivery receipt, seen indicator, blue ticks, message status

    A per-message marker reporting how far a message got, sent, delivered, or read, drawn as ticks, labels, or the recipient's avatar.

  • Reading progressalso reading progress bar, scroll progress indicator, scroll-linked progress bar, article progress bar

    A bar tied to scroll position that shows how much of an article remains, usually pinned to the top edge and filling as the reader descends.

  • Recurring eventalso recurrence rule, RRULE, repeating event

    An event stored once as a rule instead of as copies, so every second Tuesday can be edited, skipped for one week, or ended, without touching the rest.

  • Roach motelalso hard to cancel, cancellation friction, easy in hard out, click to cancel violation

    Making entry into a commitment take one click and exit take a phone call, a chat queue, or a buried page, so the asymmetry keeps people subscribed.

  • Rule builderalso condition builder, smart playlist rules

    Assembling a condition from dropdowns and values, joined by and or or, so a non-programmer can express a query without writing one.

  • Save for lateralso wishlist, saved items, read it later, favorites, watchlist

    Moving an item out of the active list into a parked list that stays associated with the reader, without deleting it or committing to it.

  • Scarcity indicatoralso low stock warning, limited availability, only N left, scarcity

    A label reporting how little of something is left, in stock, in seats, or in time, placed beside the action it is meant to accelerate.

  • Scroll edge fadealso edge fade, scroll fade, overflow fade, fade mask, scroll shadow

    A gradient mask at the edge of a scroll container that fades clipped content out, hinting that more lies past the edge without drawing a control.

  • Scroll hijackingalso scrolljacking, hijacked scrolling, scroll takeover

    Overriding the browser's scrolling so a wheel turn advances a scripted sequence instead of moving the page by the distance the reader asked for.

  • Scroll spyalso scrollspy

    Navigation that watches the scroll position and highlights the entry for the section currently in view.

  • Scrollytellingalso scroll driven narrative, pinned scroll, sticky graphic, scroll story

    A narrative layout where a pinned graphic stays fixed while text scrolls past it, each step changing what the graphic shows.

  • Select all across pagesalso select all matching, select all 1,204 items, cross-page selection

    Offering to extend a selection from the visible page to every matching row, after a header checkbox has selected only what is on screen.

  • Sender namealso from name, friendly from, display name

    The name a mail appears to come from, chosen rather than inherited, because a reader scanning an inbox decides on it before reading a word of the subject.

  • Skeleton screenalso skeleton, content placeholder

    A loading state made of grey shapes standing where the content will be, showing the layout before the data arrives.

  • Slash commandalso slash menu, inline command menu, block menu, slash command menu, slash commands

    Typing a forward slash in a composer to open a menu of actions, where choosing one runs it rather than inserting the words into the message.

  • Smart app banneralso app install banner, open in app prompt, app interstitial, install prompt

    A strip pinned to the top of a mobile web page urging the reader to open or install the native app instead, with an install or open action.

  • Smart defaultsalso good defaults, sensible defaults, prefilled values, recommended option

    Prefilling a choice with the answer most people in this situation want, so the common case needs no work and every other case is still reachable.

  • Sneak into basketalso sneaking, added extras, unwanted add-on

    Adding an item the buyer never chose, an insurance, a donation, a warranty, into the cart through a preselected control found earlier in the flow.

  • Social loginalso social sign-in, continue with Google, third-party login, OAuth login, federated login

    Signing in by delegating identity to an existing account elsewhere, presented as a row of provider buttons above or instead of a password form.

  • Social proofalso testimonials, review count, used by logos, activity notification, N people are viewing this

    Showing what other people did or said next to a decision, as counts, reviews, logos, or live activity, so the reader can borrow their judgement.

  • Soft deletealso trash, recycle bin, archive, deleted items, recently deleted

    Removing an item from view but keeping it recoverable in a trash or archive for a period, so deletion is reversible without a confirmation step.

  • Speculative loadingalso prefetching, preloading, prerendering, speculation rules, instant navigation

    Fetching, or fully rendering, a page before the reader asks for it, on a guess about where they go next, so the navigation is already done when the click lands.

  • Steps leftalso completeness meter, step 2 of 5

    Telling the reader where they are in a multi step task and how many steps are still to come, so the end of the task is visible from the start.

  • Sticky add to cartalso sticky buy bar, persistent add to cart, floating purchase bar, sticky CTA

    A condensed product bar that pins itself to an edge once the real buy button has scrolled away, keeping price and purchase always reachable.

  • Storiesalso story format, ephemeral stories, story rail, segmented progress bars

    A sequence of full-screen cards that advance on a timer, paused by holding, skipped by tapping the edges, and gone after a fixed lifetime.

  • Streakalso daily streak, streak counter, streak freeze, chain

    A count of consecutive periods in which the reader did the thing, displayed prominently and lost if a period is missed.

  • Structured formatalso split field, multi-part input, date input three fields

    Splitting one value across several small fields with fixed shapes, which teaches the expected format but costs the reader extra focus moves.

  • Styled alt textalso alt text styling, image blocking fallback

    Alt text given size, colour and weight so a mail whose images are blocked still reads as something designed instead of as a column of grey stubs.

  • Subject linealso subject, email subject

    The one line a mail is opened or ignored on, written to survive a list that truncates it and shows it wedged between the sender and the preview text.

  • Swipe deckalso card stack, tinder swipe, swipe cards, yes no deck

    A stack of cards judged one at a time, each thrown left or right to record a binary decision and reveal the next card underneath.

  • Swipe to dismissalso swipe away, dismissible, flick to dismiss, swipe to clear

    Flinging an item off the edge of the screen to remove it, with the item tracking the finger and snapping back if the throw is too short.

  • Table of contentsalso contents, in-page navigation

    A list of a document's sections, linked in reading order, shown beside or above the text.

  • Time zone displayalso local time, secondary time zone, time zone selector
