surface

The finish on a shape: its corners, its edges, its depth, its texture.

21 terms · all categories

Tagged in this category: depth11 · retro8 · illustration6 · tokens4 · perception3 · editorial2

  • Bevel and embossalso beveled edge, emboss, outset border, 3D border, chiseled edge

    Light and dark edges drawn along a shape's border so it reads as raised or carved, the depth trick behind classic desktop buttons.

  • Chamferalso chamfered corner, cut corner, angled corner, clipped corner

    A corner cut flat at an angle instead of rounded, the sharp-edged sibling of border-radius that reads industrial, military, or sci-fi.

  • Concentric corner radiusalso nested corner radius, corner concentricity, concentricity, nested radius

    Sizing a nested shape's corners so its curve shares a centre with the one outside it, the inner radius being the outer radius less the padding between them.

  • Corner radiusalso border-radius, rounded corners, corner rounding, shape scale

    How far a shape's corners are rounded off, measured as the radius of the circle that replaces the square angle, and the one number that decides how soft a UI reads.

  • Dot grid backgroundalso dot pattern, grid pattern, graph paper background, dotted background, blueprint grid

    A faint repeating field of dots or ruled squares behind content, borrowed from graph paper and canvas tools to suggest an editable surface rather than a page.

  • Elevationalso depth, shadow level, z-height

    How high a surface sits above the one behind it, expressed as a shadow, a tint, or both, and held to a few named steps so that height means something.

  • Glossalso glossy button, shine

    A wet plastic finish made from a light gradient across the top half of a control, a highlight arc, and a darker lower half.

  • Gradient borderalso gradient outline, gradient stroke, rainbow border, animated border, glow border, shine border

    An outline filled with a gradient rather than a single color, usually built from a conic or linear gradient behind a masked shape so the hue travels around the edge.

  • Grainy gradientalso noise texture, grain overlay, film grain, noisy background, feTurbulence background

    A gradient with a fine noise layer over it, added to hide banding and give a flat background the texture of printed paper or film.

  • Halftonealso dot screen, Ben Day dots, comic dots, newsprint effect

    Rendering tone as a grid of dots that vary in size, borrowed from print and used to give flat interface art a comic or newsprint feel.

  • Holographic foilalso iridescent, iridescence, holo gradient, pearlescent, oil slick

    A finish that shifts hue with viewing angle, imitating foil, soap film, or a CD surface with banded rainbow gradients over a pale metallic ground.

  • Letterpress textalso engraved text, embossed text, debossed type, text-shadow trick

    A text effect that fakes stamping into the surface, using a one pixel light shadow below the glyphs and a slightly darker fill above it.

  • Long shadowalso long flat shadow, 45 degree shadow, flat long shadow

    A flat era decoration where an icon casts a single hard shadow at 45 degrees that runs far past the shape before fading out.

  • Neon glowalso glow effect, outer glow, neon text, bloom, text glow

    A saturated colour surrounded by layered soft shadows of the same hue, so type or a border reads as a lit tube against a dark surface.

  • Organic blobalso blob, organic shape, border-radius blob, amoeba shape

    A soft irregular shape with no straight edges, used as a background accent or image mask to soften a strictly rectangular layout.

  • Progressive bluralso gradient blur, graduated blur, layered blur, fade to blur, blur scrim

    A blur whose strength ramps across a region instead of applying evenly, so content fades into softness toward an edge rather than stopping at a hard line.

  • Scanlinesalso CRT effect, raster lines, screen door effect, TV lines

    Thin repeating dark lines laid over an interface to imitate the raster of a cathode ray tube screen, often with a slight curve and flicker.

  • Specular highlightalso highlight, sheen, glint

    The bright reflection drawn on a surface to say it is glossy, placed where a light source would strike a curved object.

  • Squirclealso superellipse, continuous corner radius, smooth corners, iOS icon shape, G2 continuity

    A rounded square whose corners curve continuously into the sides instead of meeting a circular arc, the shape behind iOS app icons.

  • Terrazzoalso terrazzo pattern, chip pattern, speckle pattern, confetti texture

    A background pattern of small irregular chips scattered on a plain field, copied from poured stone flooring and used flat and multicolored behind interface content.

  • Vignettealso vignetting, lens vignette, edge darkening

    A gradual darkening toward the edges of an image that pulls the eye to the centre, borrowed from lens falloff and applied deliberately for focus and mood.
