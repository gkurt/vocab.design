dragging

Picking something up, moving it, and letting it go.

43 terms across 6 categories · all tags

component

  • Bottom sheet

    A surface anchored to the bottom edge that slides up over the content, often draggable between a peeked and an expanded height.

  • Column resizer

    The invisible strip on a column border that widens or narrows the column when dragged, and often fits the column to its content on double click.

  • Drag handle

    The small gripped area, often drawn as rows of dots or lines, that marks where an item may be picked up and dragged.

  • File upload

    A control that takes files, by opening the system picker from a button or by accepting a drop into a marked area.

  • Grabber

    The short rounded bar drawn at the top of a bottom sheet to say the surface can be dragged.

  • Image comparison slider

    Two images stacked with a draggable divider that wipes between them, used to show a before and an after in one frame.

  • Image well

    An editable image view that shows its current picture and accepts a replacement dragged or pasted straight into it.

  • Knob

    A round control turned by dragging in an arc, borrowed from hardware and mostly found in audio and creative tools.

  • Playhead

    The marker showing the current position in a timeline or track, which travels during playback and can be dragged to move it.

  • Range slider

    A slider with two handles on one track that sets a lower and an upper bound, so the selection is an interval rather than a point.

  • Resize handle

    The small corner grip that lets you drag an element or window bigger and smaller, drawn as diagonal ridges on a textarea or window corner.

  • Scrubber

    The seek control of a media player: a track showing elapsed and buffered time with a playhead you drag to move through it.

  • Slider

    A control that sets a value by dragging a handle along a track, showing the whole range at once.

  • Splitter

    The draggable bar between two panes that changes how much room each one gets, keyboard operable as a slider between limits.

  • Track

    The groove a slider thumb or scrollbar travels along, usually drawn as filled before the handle and empty after it.

layout

  • Kanban board

    A row of vertical columns, each one a stage, holding cards that move sideways as work progresses.

pattern

  • Direct manipulation

    Acting on an object by handling the object itself, dragging, resizing, or typing into it, rather than describing the change in a separate control.

  • Drag preview

    The semi-transparent copy of the dragged item that follows the pointer, sometimes collapsed into a stack when several items move at once.

  • Drag to reorder

    Changing the order of a list by dragging an item to a new position, with the remaining items shifting to show where it will land.

  • Drop indicator

    The line or highlighted gap drawn during a drag to show exactly where the item will land if it is released now.

  • Pull to refresh

    Dragging a list downward past its top edge to trigger a reload, with an indicator that appears in the space the drag opens up.

  • Swipe deck

    A stack of cards judged one at a time, each thrown left or right to record a binary decision and reveal the next card underneath.

  • Swipe to dismiss

    Flinging an item off the edge of the screen to remove it, with the item tracking the finger and snapping back if the throw is too short.

interaction

  • Brushing

    Dragging a selection across one chart to highlight the same records in every linked view.

  • Dead zone

    A range of input near the resting position that is deliberately ignored, so tiny unintended movement produces no response at all.

  • Detent

    A position a control settles into and resists leaving, so a continuous drag or turn lands on discrete values that can be felt rather than read.

  • Drag and drop

    Picking up an object, moving it under continuous pointer or finger contact, and releasing it over a target that accepts it.

  • Drag autoscroll

    Scrolling a container automatically when a drag is held near its edge, so an item can be moved somewhere currently off screen.

  • Drag threshold

    The small distance a pointer must travel while pressed before the gesture counts as a drag, so a shaky click is not mistaken for one.

  • Drag to create

    Making a new object by dragging out its extent rather than pressing Add: an event drawn across two hours, a frame drawn on a canvas, a range drawn on a chart.

  • Drop zone

    The region that will accept a dragged item, usually highlighted while a compatible drag is in flight so the destination is unmistakable.

  • Fling

    The fastest of the drag family: contact is released while it is still moving, so the content keeps travelling and the gesture cannot be dragged back.

  • Lasso selection

    Selecting by dragging a boundary around items on a canvas, freehand or as a rectangle, so whatever the outline encloses becomes the selection when the drag ends.

  • Pointer capture

    Redirecting all events for one pointer to a chosen element, so a drag keeps reaching the control it started on even when it wanders off.

  • Press-drag-release

    Operating a menu in one gesture: pressing the trigger opens it, dragging moves through the items while the button stays down, and releasing over an item commits it.

  • Scrubbing

    Dragging a playhead along a timeline to move through media by hand, previewing frames as the position changes rather than waiting for playback.

  • Scrubby slider

    A numeric field whose label can be dragged sideways to change the value, giving a slider's feel without a slider taking up room.

  • Selection handle

    The draggable grip at each end of a touch text selection, used to widen or narrow the range a finger cannot adjust precisely.

  • Slide to confirm

    A control that commits only once its thumb is dragged the length of its track, so the effort of the gesture is the guard against doing it by accident.

  • Snapping

    Pulling a dragged object into exact alignment once it comes within a small distance of a grid, guide, or another object's edge.

  • Spring loading

    Hovering a dragged item over a container until it opens, so a drag can travel through nested destinations without being dropped and restarted.

motion

  • Interactive transition

    A transition whose progress is held by an ongoing gesture, so the reader can drag it partway, hesitate, and either complete it or send it back.

accessibility

  • Dragging alternative

    A second, non-dragging route to anything a drag can do, such as arrow keys or a move-to menu on a sortable list.
