tag

canvas

An unbounded workspace, panned and zoomed, with things placed on it.

24 terms across 6 categories · all tags

component

  • Flowchart

    A diagram of steps in boxes joined by arrows, read rather than operated, and now more often generated from a few lines of text than drawn by hand.

  • Minimap

    A shrunken overview of a long document or canvas beside the scrollbar, with a box showing which part is currently on screen.

  • Node graph

    A canvas of boxes wired to each other by lines, where each box does one job and the wiring between them is the thing being built, rearranged by dragging.

  • Node port

    The typed stub on a node where a wire may land, coloured or shaped by what it accepts, so a connection that would make no sense refuses to complete.

layout

  • Edge routing

    How a connector gets from one node to another: a bezier sweep, a right-angled staircase, or a straight line, picked so the crossings stay readable.

  • Fisheye view

    A focus plus context display that magnifies whatever has attention and progressively compresses everything further away, like a lens dragged along a list.

  • Focus plus context

    A layout that keeps one region in full detail inside its own surroundings, shrinking or dimming the context instead of moving it into a second pane.

  • Graph layout

    Placing the nodes of a wired diagram by algorithm rather than by hand: layered for a flow, radial for a hub, force-directed for a mesh that has no direction.

  • Infinite canvas

    A workspace with no page edges, where content is placed anywhere in an unbounded plane and the viewport pans and zooms over it instead of scrolling a document.

  • Overview plus detail

    A layout that shows a compressed overview and a detailed view at the same time in separate regions, so the reader keeps their place while inspecting one part.

  • Semantic zoom

    Switching between two views of the same content, a zoomed out view of group headings and a zoomed in view of individual items, instead of scaling pixels.

pattern

  • Direct manipulation

    Acting on an object by handling the object itself, dragging, resizing, or typing into it, rather than describing the change in a separate control.

interaction

  • Brushing

    Dragging a selection across one chart to highlight the same records in every linked view.

  • Drag to create

    Making a new object by dragging out its extent rather than pressing Add: an event drawn across two hours, a frame drawn on a canvas, a range drawn on a chart.

  • Lasso selection

    Selecting by dragging a boundary around items on a canvas, freehand or as a rectangle, so whatever the outline encloses becomes the selection when the drag ends.

  • Nudge

    Moving or changing a value by one small fixed step with an arrow key, with a modifier switching to a larger step.

  • Orbit

    Rotating a camera around a fixed point by dragging, so the scene appears to turn while the subject stays centred.

  • Pan

    Moving the viewport across content in any direction while keeping contact, so the content feels anchored to the finger or cursor.

  • Pinch to zoom

    Two fingers moving apart or together on a surface to scale content up or down, with the scale tracking the distance between them.

  • Rotate gesture

    Two fingers turning around a common center to rotate the object or view beneath them, the angle tracking the fingers as they orbit.

  • Smart guides

    Alignment lines that appear only while dragging, showing which edges or centers the moving object currently lines up with.

  • Snapping

    Pulling a dragged object into exact alignment once it comes within a small distance of a grid, guide, or another object's edge.

motion

  • Marching ants

    The animated dashed outline that circles a selection, moving continuously so the boundary reads as active rather than drawn.

surface

  • Dot grid background

    A faint repeating field of dots or ruled squares behind content, borrowed from graph paper and canvas tools to suggest an editable surface rather than a page.
