microinteraction

The loops that are kinds of one, and the ripples and toasts that are parts of one.

16 terms across 5 categories · all tags

Microinteraction is also a term in its own right. Its page carries the definition, a demo, and these same members grouped by how they relate to it.

component

  • Copy button

    A small control beside a value that copies it to the clipboard and confirms by turning into a tick for a second or two.

  • Password reveal toggle

    The control inside a password field, usually an eye or the word Show, that switches the characters between masked and readable.

  • Toast

    A brief, self-dismissing message that appears at a screen edge to confirm an action or report status without interrupting the user's flow.

pattern

  • Inline validation

    Checking a field as the reader fills or leaves it and showing the verdict beside that field, rather than saving every complaint for the submit button.

  • Optimistic UI

    Showing the result of an action immediately and reconciling with the server afterward, instead of making the user wait for confirmation.

  • Pull to refresh

    Dragging a list downward past its top edge to trigger a reload, with an indicator that appears in the space the drag opens up.

  • Swipe to dismiss

    Flinging an item off the edge of the screen to remove it, with the item tracking the finger and snapping back if the throw is too short.

  • Type to confirm

    Guarding a destructive action by making the reader type the name of the thing being destroyed before the confirm button becomes usable.

interaction

  • Auditory icon

    A recorded everyday sound standing for an event, so a crumple means delete by resembling the act rather than by convention learned in the interface.

  • Earcon

    A brief synthesized sound standing for an event, abstract by design, so a rising figure means success only because the interface taught you it does.

  • Haptic feedback

    A brief physical vibration or tap produced by the device to confirm an interaction, mark a boundary, or signal success and failure.

  • Hold to confirm

    Requiring a press to be held for a moment before a destructive action commits, with progress shown so the wait is understood as deliberate.

  • Slide to confirm

    A control that commits only once its thumb is dragged the length of its track, so the effort of the gesture is the guard against doing it by accident.

motion

  • Ripple

    A circular wash that spreads from the point of contact to acknowledge a touch, Material's way of tying feedback to where the finger landed.

  • Yellow fade technique

    Washing a just-changed element in colour and letting it fade back out, so a change made without a page reload is still noticed where it happened.

color

  • State layer

    A translucent sheet of the content colour laid over a control at a fixed opacity per state, so hover, focus and pressed are one rule instead of three palettes.
